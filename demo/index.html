Clear space

Preserve clear space equal to the height of the “a” on all four sides of the wordmark. Never place other elements, text, or edges inside that zone.

Rules

Do
  • Use the primary wordmark on light backgrounds.
  • Use the inverse wordmark on Ink or photography.
  • Use the lettermark below 24px of height.
  • Keep the logo on solid or gently graded backgrounds.
Don't
  • Do not stretch, rotate, or distort the logo.
  • Do not recolor the wordmark outside the palette.
  • Do not add shadows, outlines, or effects.
  • Do not place the wordmark over busy imagery.

Photography

Calm, natural textures over stocky office scenes. Photography sets mood, never narrates the product — the product speaks through the interface.

FOREST CANOPY
MORNING FOG
RIVER STONE

Style direction

SubjectSingle natural subject, centered, calm. Never a crowd, never an office.
LightSoft, directional, early-morning. No hard flash, no HDR.
PaletteGreens and warm neutrals that echo the brand palette.
ReferenceForest edges, fog over fields, river stones, moss on bark — texture at a human distance.

Rules

Do
  • Single subject per frame.
  • Keep at least a third of the frame quiet for type.
  • Grade toward the brand greens.
Don't
  • No stock office scenes or posed teams.
  • No aerial drone shots.
  • No saturated, high-contrast grading.

Messaging

How Arden sounds — in headlines, product copy, and posts. This section is what downstream tools read first.

Voice

Calm, specific, quietly confident.
Second person (“you”), active voice, short sentences. Name the workflow, name the outcome, skip the adjectives. Arden never sounds excited; it sounds certain.
Avoid
Generic buzzwords“Revolutionary / game-changing”Exclamation marks
Prefer
Specific workflowsConcrete time savedPlain verbs

Headline formula

[Manual pain] → [What now runs itself]
Your team spends the first week of every deal on setup. Arden makes it an afternoon.
Supporting copy: one concrete workflow, one number, one proof point — never more than three sentences. CTA: action verb plus a time horizon (“See your onboarding mapped in 15 minutes”). Avoid “Learn more”.

Rules

Do
  • Lead with the outcome, support with the mechanism.
  • Use real numbers from real runs.
  • Write like you'd explain it to one founder over coffee.
Don't
  • Don't claim AI magic without a concrete mechanism.
  • Don't invent metrics or customers.
  • Don't stack more than one idea per sentence.
