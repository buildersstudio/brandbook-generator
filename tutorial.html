How to use

This site is what the tool produces. The tool itself is this repo — it runs inside Claude Code on your machine, and your brand never leaves it unless you publish.

1

Clone the repo

You need Claude Code installed. Then:

git clone https://github.com/buildersstudio/brandbook-generator.git cd brandbook-generator claude
2

Point it at your brand

Tell Claude what carries your brand — a website, a pitch deck, a folder of screenshots and logos. Anything works as input.

My website is acme.example — build my brandbook.
3

Let it distill

Claude extracts your logo, palette, type system, spacing, photography style, and voice, writes them into one structured brand-book.json, and renders a site like this one — in your colors and your type.

4

Iterate in plain words

React to what you see and Claude edits the book.

The green is wrong — use the darker one from our logo. And our voice is warmer than this.
5

Publish it

The book lives on localhost by default. When you're ready, ask Claude to publish it to a public link — Vercel or GitHub Pages — so your team and your tools can point at it.

Publish my brandbook so I can share it.

Why publish

A published brandbook becomes the source of truth every tool in the Builders toolkit reads from — generate a LinkedIn post and it already knows your colors, your fonts, your voice, and which claims are real. It is also your marketing repository: one link that answers every “what's our hex / font / tagline” question.
