The wordmark opens with the four-pointed spark. Black on cream, white on deep ink. Nothing in between.

Clear space

Preserve clear space equal to the height of the spark on all four sides. On photography, place the white wordmark over the darkest region of the frame or over a gradient scrim.

Rules

Do
  • Black wordmark on cream and light surfaces.
  • White wordmark on deep ink, photography, gradients.
  • Spark alone in small spaces.
  • Always 100% opacity.
Don't
  • No stretching, rotating, or distorting.
  • No recoloring into the accent palette.
  • No shadows, outlines, or glows.
  • No busy photography without a scrim.
