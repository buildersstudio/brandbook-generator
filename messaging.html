Messaging

How Builders sounds — on the site, in posts, at events. This chapter is what downstream tools read first.

Voice

Direct, confident, builder-to-builder.
Short declaratives. Concrete numbers and dates. The studio talks like a founder who has shipped, not like a fund that observes. One serif-italic flourish per asset — the emphasis, never the whole line.
Avoid
Hype adjectivesVague timelinesFund-speak
Prefer
Concrete datesNamed eventsPlain verbs

Headline formula

[KICKER IN DM MONO] + [Declarative in Stack Sans Headline 700, one serif-italic emphasis]
Where bold founders build stellar companies.
Supporting copy: what happens, when, and for whom — three sentences at most. CTA: a date beats an adjective (“Applications close August 15” over “Apply now”).

Rules

Do
  • Lead with what happens and when.
  • Real numbers, real dates, real events.
  • One serif-italic flourish per asset, at most.
Don't
  • Banned phrasings: founder counts, “one crew”, “side by side”, “prove it in ten weeks”, “the work is the application”.
  • Don't bake a countdown and post it days later.
  • Don't stack more than one idea per sentence.
